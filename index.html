How the assistant decides

It checks how much came out, how long it took, and how it tasted. Grind is the main dial: it changes only the grind, by a size that matches how far off the shot was. When the clock and your tongue disagree, your tongue wins. Temperature is the fine-tuner, used once timing is close.

Practice: taste two shots

Pull one shot stopped at 1:1.5 and one run to 1:3 with the same grind. Sip them side by side. The short one teaches you sour; the long one teaches you bitter and dry. Your own recipe sits somewhere between them.
